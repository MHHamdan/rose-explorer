Generative AI, explained and verified

How generative AI works,
one knob at a time.

Every generative model, from a VAE to a large language model to a text-to-image system, must get around one obstacle: the probability of the data is intractable. The route it takes fixes its objective, its sampler and the evaluations it admits. Move the knobs below and watch the consequences, then open the notebooks to see each equation derived and checked in code.

The ROSE chain

The survey follows every model through the same five questions, in order. Each tab of the explorer and each notebook chapter sits on one of them.

  1. 1

    Route

    How a model gets around the intractable likelihood: compute it exactly, bound it, or avoid it.

    autoregressive · flows · VAEs · diffusion · GANs

  2. 2

    Objective

    The route fixes the loss and the divergence it minimises, and so which data the model covers.

    forward KL · reverse KL · Jensen-Shannon · Wasserstein · Fisher

  3. 3

    Sampler

    The same trained weights can be sampled many ways; the sampler sets quality, diversity and cost.

    greedy · top-p · Langevin · DDPM · DDIM · guidance

  4. 4

    Evaluation

    What a model can report decides which metrics are admissible and what they cannot see.

    bits/dim · perplexity · FID · precision/recall · human judgments

  5. 5

    Systems

    Post-training, retrieval and agents recombine the routes; each stage is anchored to the last.

    SFT · RLHF · DPO · GRPO · RAG · agents

How it is verified

Every equation has a check

The 205 entries of the paper's equation registry are covered by 111 notebook entries and roughly 400 numeric or symbolic checks. A notebook fails loudly if any check fails.

The notebooks are generated

Each notebook is built from a Python module under notebooks/src/. The repository's gate rebuilds them, executes them on a CPU and fails if a committed notebook drifts.

The explorer uses the same mathematics

The demos compute their figures live from the equations the notebooks verify; the numbers under each figure come from the same computation.
